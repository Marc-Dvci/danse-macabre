CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, subject 05, trial 12
Start with arms down by your sides, step in place on the left foot.
4 counts per bar, 100 counts per minute.
Bar 1, count 2: left leg lifts backward, low, over 2 counts.
Count 3: step in place on the left foot, knees bent, over 13 counts.
Bar 4, count 4: left arm moves forward and to your left, high, over 1 count.
Count 4 and: right leg lifts backward and to your left, low, over half a count.
Bar 5, count 1: left arm moves to your left, high, over 1 count; right arm moves forward, low, over half a count.
Count 1 and: right leg lifts backward, low, over half a count; right arm moves to your right, middle, over 1 count.
Count 2 and: left arm moves down by your side, over 1 count.
Count 3: right leg lifts backward and to your left, low, over half a count.
